
# NexaSoft — Cancellation and Refund Policy

## Project Cancellation
Clients may request project cancellation
by contacting hello@nexasoft.example.

## Before Development Begins
Payments may be refundable after deducting
any mutually agreed non-refundable expenses.

## After Development Begins
Refund requests are reviewed based on
completed work, agreed milestones, and
contractual obligations.

## Non-Refundable Costs
Approved third-party licenses, subscriptions,
and external service fees may be non-refundable.

## Refund Requests
Customers should email
hello@nexasoft.example with their
project details and reason for requesting
a refund.

## Important Note
Refund eligibility is determined by the
individual project agreement. Refunds are
not automatically guaranteed.
