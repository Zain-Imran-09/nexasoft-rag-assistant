
# NexaSoft — Client Onboarding

## Step 1: Initial Inquiry
Clients contact hello@nexasoft.example
to discuss their project.

## Step 2: Free Consultation
NexaSoft conducts an initial consultation
to understand the client's requirements.

## Step 3: Project Proposal
NexaSoft prepares a proposal describing
the scope, estimated costs, and timeline.

## Step 4: Agreement
Both parties approve the project scope,
payment milestones, and delivery terms.

## Step 5: Project Kickoff
The development team begins work after
the initial 30% payment is received.

## Step 6: Progress Updates
Clients receive progress updates during
agreed project milestones.

## Step 7: Delivery and Acceptance
The final deliverable is reviewed by the
client before acceptance and final payment.
