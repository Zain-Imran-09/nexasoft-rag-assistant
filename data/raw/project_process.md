
# NexaSoft — Software Development Process

## Discovery
The team gathers requirements and defines
the business problem.

## Planning
The project scope, timeline, and technical
approach are documented.

## Design
The team prepares wireframes,
user experience designs, and architecture.

## Development
Developers implement the approved features.

## Testing
The team tests software functionality,
reliability, and expected behavior.

## Deployment
Approved software is deployed to the
agreed environment.

## Maintenance
Post-deployment support is provided
according to the client's agreement.

## Methodology
NexaSoft generally follows an Agile,
iterative development approach.
