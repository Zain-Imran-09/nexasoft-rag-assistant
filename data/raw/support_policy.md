
# NexaSoft — Customer Support Policy

## Support Channels
Customers can contact NexaSoft through email
at hello@nexasoft.example.

## Support Hours
Monday to Friday, 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM PKT.

Regular support is not available on weekends.

## Response Time Targets
Critical issues: Within 4 business hours.

High-priority issues: Within 8 business hours.

General questions: Within 2 business days.

These are response targets, not guaranteed
resolution times.

## Support Coverage
Support services may include bug fixes,
troubleshooting, and technical guidance.

## Maintenance Agreements
Ongoing maintenance and support are available
under separately agreed service contracts.
